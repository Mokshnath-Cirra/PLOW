Treatment & Care Plan
Urgent Priority
STEP 1 • PHYSICAL CONTAINMENT

Prune Infected Lower Leaves

Using sterilized shears (70% isopropyl alcohol), clip all foliage exhibiting concentric bullseye spots or chlorotic yellow halos. Place clippings directly into a sealed bag to prevent airborne spore drift.

STEP 2 • SPRAY PRESCRIPTION

Apply Liquid Copper Fungicide

Spray organic OMRI-listed Liquid Copper Octanoate or Bacillus subtilis spray in late evening to avoid leaf burn. Thoroughly coat both the upper and lower leaf surfaces.

Dosage: 2 fl oz (4 tbsp) per gallon of water. Repeat every 7 days until clear.
